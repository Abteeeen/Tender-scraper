02

What's locked

The doors the free system can't open by itself.

We can see every tender listing. What we can't reach on the free tier is what sits behind a login — and that's exactly the material needed to bid and to reach the right person.

▣ LOCKED

The full tender pack

VendorPanel, Tenders.Net and eProcure require an account and often "register interest" before the PDFs, pricing sheets and specs unlock. A plain fetch can't cross that.

▣ LOCKED

Terms & conditions gates

Some portals force a checkbox / T&C acceptance or a captcha per download — a deliberate barrier to automation.

▣ LOCKED

The decision-maker's socials

The tender names a contact officer, but their LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram — the warm outreach channels — live behind those networks, not in the document.

▣ LOCKED

Missing contact details

When a listing omits a direct email or phone, filling that gap needs an external lookup the free tier doesn't run.

03

Why a human is in the loop now

One person crosses the locked doors — for now.

The free system does all the finding, reading, tiering, zone-filtering and drafting. A person is needed for the single stretch the free tier can't automate: logging into the portal and pulling the pack.

01

System captures & qualifies

Every in-zone security/cleaning tender is scored A–C, drafted, and logged — automatically.

02

Human approves & unlocks

The reviewer marks the good ones Approved, logs into the portal, downloads the pack, and drops the link in. This is the only manual step.

03

System resumes

Deep analysis, compliance, pricing, bid strategy and the point-of-contact extraction all run automatically from there.

Free path vs paid path

What the paid layer unlocks.

Free path · running today

Built on free tools

n8n · Gemini · Google Sheets · Slack
  • ✓ Scans AusTender, VendorPanel & BCC hourly
  • ✓ AI tiering + 50 km zone filter
  • ✓ First-pass bid draft per tender
  • ✓ Deep analysis of uploaded packs
  • ✓ Point of contact from the tender text (buyer, officer, email, phone)
  • ✕ Can't auto-download login-gated packs
  • ✕ No LinkedIn / Facebook / Instagram
  • ✕ Needs a human to unlock the portal
Paid path · fully automated

+ Apify social & download layer

everything free, plus the locked doors
  • ★ Auto-login & auto-download from VendorPanel & portals
  • ★ Social scraper — verified LinkedIn / Facebook / Instagram
  • ★ Fills missing email / phone via external lookup
  • ★ Auto-approve Tier A in-zone (no human tick)
  • ★ Expands to QTenders, Moreton Bay & more councils
  • ★ Fallback alert only when a portal truly blocks a bot
  • ★ A ready-to-contact lead card per tender
  • ★ Hands-off end to end
The paid layer is modular — you switch it on source by source, so cost scales with the value it returns.
05

So — can we go forward?

Yes. The engine is proven; the paid layer is a switch, not a rebuild.

Everything above the locked doors already works. Moving to fully automated means plugging Apify into three places — portal download, social/contact enrichment, and auto-approval — while the rest of the pipeline stays exactly as it is today.
