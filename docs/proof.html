A

Maintenance of Access Control, CCTV, Duress & Nurse Call Systems

Security · Electronic/CCTV Statewide QLD Closes 16 Jul, 2:00 PM Ongoing · 3 yr + 2×1 yr
Compliance requirements

Current QLD Security Firm & individual Security licences; ASIAL membership preferred; Cabling Provider Rules registration for electronic works; Public Liability $20M; Professional Indemnity $5M; Workcover QLD; National Police Certificate for all field staff.

Pricing structure

Schedule of rates: fixed monthly preventative-maintenance fee per site band, hourly corrective-maintenance rates (business / after-hours), call-out fee, and a parts margin cap. GST exclusive. Annual CPI escalation from year 2.

Scope of work

Preventative and corrective maintenance of access-control, CCTV, duress and nurse-call systems across multiple health facilities. Includes scheduled inspections, fault rectification, 24/7 emergency call-out, spare-parts management and compliance reporting. Sites operate around the clock — response windows apply.

KPIs & SLAs

Priority-1 emergency response ≤ 15 minutes phone / ≤ 2 hours on-site; Priority-2 ≤ 24 hours; 98% preventative-maintenance schedule adherence; monthly performance reporting; escalation matrix for critical outages.

Submission & evaluation

Online lodgement via portal; capability statement (≤ 10 pages), 3 referees, licence evidence, WHS system. Evaluated on price (30%), capability & experience (30%), local response (20%), WHS & compliance (20%).

Recommended bid strategy

An incumbent likely holds this contract — differentiate on local SEQ presence and guaranteed response times, not price alone. Emphasise licensed in-house technicians, a 15-minute emergency call-out commitment, and health-sector experience. Address the 24/7 nature directly with a named after-hours escalation team. Keep pricing competitive on the monthly PM fee where evaluation weight sits.
