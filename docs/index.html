FAQ

Questions your team will ask.

Why is a human still in the loop today?

One reason only: tender documents on portals like VendorPanel sit behind account logins and "register interest" gates that a free tool can't cross. A reviewer approves the good tenders and drops in the pack — everything before and after that click is automatic. The paid Autopilot layer removes this step.

What exactly is locked behind logins?

The full tender packs (PDFs, pricing spreadsheets, specifications), portals that require a T&C acceptance or captcha per download, and the decision-maker's social profiles. The listings themselves are public — which is why the free engine can find and rank everything. See the coverage map for the full picture.

Does the AI ever invent contact details?

No — this is a hard rule in the system. Names, emails and phone numbers are extracted only when they're explicitly written in the tender document, verbatim. If a detail isn't there, the field stays blank, and the paid layer finds it from verified external sources instead of guessing.

What is Apify, and why is that part paid?

Apify is a professional scraping platform that runs real browser sessions — it can log into portals, accept terms, download packs, and search social networks. Those capabilities cost compute per run, which is why they sit in the paid layer. It's modular: we switch it on portal by portal, so you only pay for what returns value.

Which tender sources are covered?

Live today: AusTender (federal), VendorPanel (the shared hub for Gold Coast, Logan, Ipswich, Redland and QLD state government) and Brisbane City Council. Planned: QTenders and City of Moreton Bay. Because most SEQ councils share VendorPanel, authenticating it once unlocks the majority of in-zone tenders in a single move.

What happens when a portal blocks automation?

Nothing stalls silently. If a portal throws a captcha or a mandatory manual step, Signal sends a precise Slack alert with the exact link and the exact action needed — a person finishes that one download and the pipeline resumes automatically.
