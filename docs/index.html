Roadmap

From assisted to fully automated.

What's live today already removes the heavy lifting. The next phases close the remaining manual gaps.

Live now

Assisted pipeline

Automated discovery, AI triage, zone filtering, first-pass drafts, deep analysis and contact extraction — with a one-click human approval in the middle.

Phase 2

Hands-off intake & social enrichment

Top-tier in-zone tenders are auto-approved, packs are pulled from each portal automatically, and the buyer's socials are found — removing the manual login and download step.

Portal downloads sit behind logins and terms of use. We automate them with stored credentials, with a fallback alert whenever a portal genuinely needs a human — so nothing stalls silently.
Phase 3

Bid drafting in your voice, into your systems

Trained on Chief Group's past submissions, the AI produces complete draft responses in your tone and format, delivered straight into your CRM or bid folder.
