The bottleneck

The opportunity is real — but it's a speed problem, not a sourcing problem.

The Australian tender landscape is fragmented across federal (AusTender), aggregator (VendorPanel) and local-council portals. New security and cleaning contracts appear every day, but finding them means manually checking multiple portals, opening every listing, and reading 100-page packs just to decide whether an opportunity is worth pursuing.

By the time a person has done that across every source, the best-positioned competitor is often already drafting. The winning edge isn't access to tenders — everyone has that. It's the hours between a tender going live and a qualified team deciding to bid.

How it works today

Two AI stages, one human checkpoint.

The system runs as two connected pipelines. The first finds and qualifies every opportunity automatically. A team member gives a one-click go-ahead on the ones worth pursuing. The second then performs a deep, bid-focused read of the full tender pack.

Stage 1

Discovery & Triage

runs hourly
  • 01Scan every source — AusTender, VendorPanel and Brisbane City Council are pulled in parallel and normalised into one clean list.
  • 02Keyword filter removes obvious noise, keeping security, guarding, patrol, CCTV, cleaning and facilities work.
  • 03Never see the same tender twice — a memory of thousands of past listings suppresses duplicates automatically.
  • 04AI reads & classifies each one — service type, location, budget, contract length, hours, deadline, red flags, and an A / B / C / Reject priority tier.
  • 05Service-zone gate — an authoritative list of ~600 QLD suburbs and postcodes keeps only work inside your 50 km reach and drops everything interstate.
  • 06Draft response written — a first-pass Expression of Interest is generated for each qualified tender.
  • 07Logged to the triage sheet, ranked and ready for review.
↓
Human checkpoint

One decision, from your team. A reviewer scans the ranked sheet and marks the tenders worth pursuing as Approved. That single tick is the trigger for the deep-analysis stage — the person stays in control of what the business commits to, without doing any of the searching or reading.

↓
Stage 2

Deep Analysis & Bid Intelligence

on approval
  • 01Opens the full tender pack — PDFs, requirement and pricing spreadsheets, even zipped bundles.
  • 02AI extracts what wins bids — compliance and licensing requirements, the pricing/rate structure, full scope of work, KPIs & SLAs, submission rules and evaluation weightings.
  • 03Recommends a bid strategy — practical positioning advice tailored to the tender.
  • 04Alerts the team on Slack — the moment analysis is ready, or if a document is still needed.

Where it goes next

The roadmap: from assisted to fully automated.

What's live today already removes the heavy lifting. The next phases close the remaining manual gaps — so the system moves from finding tenders for your team to preparing complete, submission-ready bids on its own.

Live now

Assisted pipeline

Automated discovery, AI triage, zone filtering, first-pass drafts and deep bid analysis — with a one-click human approval in the middle.

Phase 2

Hands-off intake & auto-qualification

Top-tier, in-zone tenders are auto-approved by confidence rules, and the full tender pack is pulled from each portal automatically — removing the manual log-in, download and upload step entirely.

Portal downloads sit behind account log-ins and terms of use. We automate them with securely stored credentials and per-portal handling, with a fallback alert whenever a portal needs human sign-off — so nothing silently stalls.

Phase 3

Bid drafting in your voice, into your systems

Trained on Chief Group's past submissions, the AI produces complete draft responses in your tone and format, delivered straight into your CRM or bid folder — leaving your team to refine and submit, not build from scratch.
